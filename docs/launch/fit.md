# Nothing cut off

Made by `python3 tools/fit.py` in headless Chromium: every screen at the phone (390×844), the Fold open (820×1000), a phone on its side (844×390), a small Mac window (1024×700) and a big one (1440×900), in English and in French with Larger words. A screen fails on a button or link off the edge of the screen (with nothing to scroll it in), words cut off by their box, two buttons overlapping or one under something else, or a button smaller than 24 × 24 px (WCAG 2.2 target size). Screens that scroll (Settings, the Store, How to play, Game over) are checked a screen at a time down to the bottom.
