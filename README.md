# Ayo & Nchò: Nigerian Mancala

The Nigerian seed-and-board game (Ayo among the Yoruba, Nchò/Okwe among the Igbo) in real 3D, for phone and computer.

- `engine/` — rules and the computer opponent (shared by browser and server)
- `web/` — the game (Three.js board; physics and screens arrive per `docs/build-plan.md`; `online.js` is the board's end of a room: Home → With friends opens a room with a four-letter code (Invite on WhatsApp, copy or share the link, or join a friend's room by code); the friend knocks with their name and the host lets them in before they take the seat; the invite link `/?room=CODE` goes straight to the board knocking; `shouts.js` is the fixed phrases friends shout across the board; `daily.js` keeps your record for the daily puzzle (engine/puzzle.js makes the same position for everyone from the date; Home → the Daily puzzle card, or `/?puzzle=1`); `keys.js` is keyboard play — 1–6 pick your pits near to far, Enter sows, U undoes, H hints, Esc puts a pit back — and the pits' screen-reader labels; `record.js` keeps your record against each opponent — wins, losses, draws, your best win and your current run — shown under their name on Home and on the Game over card, where they have a word for a run or your first win over them; `path.js` is where the seeds will go — on the first tap, the hand's path read from the engine's own events: a brass mark on every pit it would reach, a copper ring on each capture and a light where the turn ends, with the words in the hint line; and their move, explained — after the opponent's turn (the computer's or a friend's) the same marks stay faint for a moment with the words under the board, and a tap on the words replays just that turn slowly; Settings → Feel → Show where seeds go turns both off)
- `server/` — Express plus the online rooms (`rooms.js`: a four-letter code, two seats, a door the host answers (knock / let in / not now), every move checked by the engine, reconnect by token, each move sent as a few dozen bytes that the board rebuilds with the shared engine; WebSockets at `/ws`, protocol in `index.js`)
- `npm test` — engine, rooms, server and online-client tests · `npm start` — serve locally on :3000 · `python3 tools/render.py out.png --online` — two headless phones go through the Rooms screen, knock, get let in and play a move each through the real server · `--puzzle` — plays today's daily puzzle (a miss, Try again, then the answer) and checks the result card and the Home card · `--smoke --keys` — plays from the keyboard too and checks the pits' labels · `--record` — a phone with a record against all three and a saved game one move from a third win in a row over Mama Ngozi: checks the Home pills, plays the winning move and checks the Game over card's record line and her word · `--smoke --path` — one tap on the pit with the richest path: checks a mark lands on every pit the hand would reach, the captures are ringed, the end pit lit and the hint line says where it ends, then Esc clears them and the setting off shows none
