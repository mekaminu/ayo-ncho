# Ayo & Nchò: Nigerian Mancala

The Nigerian seed-and-board game (Ayo among the Yoruba, Nchò/Okwe among the Igbo) in real 3D, for phone and computer.

- `engine/` — rules and the computer opponent (shared by browser and server)
- `web/` — the game (Three.js board; physics and screens arrive per `docs/build-plan.md`; `online.js` is the board's end of a room: Home → With friends opens a room with a four-letter code (Invite on WhatsApp, copy or share the link, or join a friend's room by code); the friend knocks with their name and the host lets them in before they take the seat; the invite link `/?room=CODE` goes straight to the board knocking; `shouts.js` is the fixed phrases friends shout across the board; `daily.js` keeps your record for the daily puzzle (engine/puzzle.js makes the same position for everyone from the date; Home → the Daily puzzle card, or `/?puzzle=1`); `keys.js` is keyboard play — 1–6 pick your pits near to far, Enter sows, U undoes, H hints, Esc puts a pit back — and the pits' screen-reader labels)
- `server/` — Express plus the online rooms (`rooms.js`: a four-letter code, two seats, a door the host answers (knock / let in / not now), every move checked by the engine, reconnect by token, each move sent as a few dozen bytes that the board rebuilds with the shared engine; WebSockets at `/ws`, protocol in `index.js`)
- `npm test` — engine, rooms, server and online-client tests · `npm start` — serve locally on :3000 · `python3 tools/render.py out.png --online` — two headless phones go through the Rooms screen, knock, get let in and play a move each through the real server · `--puzzle` — plays today's daily puzzle (a miss, Try again, then the answer) and checks the result card and the Home card · `--smoke --keys` — plays from the keyboard too and checks the pits' labels
