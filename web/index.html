    How to play Nchò

    • Your six pits are the column on the right. Tap one with seeds to pick them all up.
    • They are sown one per pit anticlockwise: up your side, then down the other side.
    • If your last seed lands in a pit that has seeds, you pick that pit up and keep going. Your turn ends when the last seed lands in an empty pit.
    • Any pit that reaches 4 seeds is taken by the owner of that side — whoever sowed it.
    • Except: if your last seed makes a pit on their side 4, you take it.
    • With 8 seeds left, whoever makes the next capture takes them all. Most seeds wins.
    • Your six pits are the column on the right. Tap one with seeds to pick them all up.
    • They are sown one per pit anticlockwise: up your side, then down the other side.
    • If your last seed makes an opponent's pit 2 or 3, you capture it, and every pit behind it that is also 2 or 3.
    • If the other side is empty, you must give them seeds when you can.
    • First to 25 seeds wins.
